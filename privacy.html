Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 26, 2026

At ADHIKAR'26, we are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our delegates, partners, and visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act), 2023.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information directly from you when you register for the event. This includes:

  • Personal Details: Name, Email Address, Phone Number.
  • Institutional Details: School/College Name, Year of Study.
  • Parental Information: Name and Phone Number of parent/guardian (mandatory for participants under 18).
  • Event Specifics: Portfolio preferences and dietary requirements (if applicable).

2. Purpose of Data Collection

Your data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • To manage your registration and participation in ADHIKAR'26.
  • To allocate portfolios and committees.
  • To issue official certificates and awards.
  • To communicate essential event updates, schedules, and logistics.
  • To ensure the safety and security of all participants on-site.

3. Children's Privacy (Under 18)

In accordance with the DPDP Act 2023, ADHIKAR'26 requires verifiable parental or guardian consent for the collection and processing of data for any individual under the age of 18. We do not engage in behavioral tracking or targeted advertising directed at students.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Information is shared with third-party service providers only when necessary to facilitate event operations:

  • EmailJS: To send automated verification and communication emails.
  • Venue Management: Providing basic attendee lists for security and accommodation logistics.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access or loss. Your personal information will be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes of the event and legal requirements, after which it will be securely deleted or anonymized.

6. Your Rights

You (or your parent/guardian) have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access and review your data.
  • The right to correct or update inaccurate information.
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time (which may affect your participation in the event).
  • The right to request the erasure of your data post-event.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, grievances, or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact the Secretariat at:

Email: adhikar.hubli@gmail.com
Address: Shubhodaya Convention Hall, Hubballi.
